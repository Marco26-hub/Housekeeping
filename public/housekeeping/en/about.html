Our Story

From one villa, to a network of premium properties

The Blondes Cleaning was born in Sirmione, on Lake Garda, from the awareness that luxury short-term rentals require an operational standard most traditional cleaning companies cannot guarantee.

We started with a single villa. An international owner who wanted continuity, discretion and quality — not a commodity. From there, in three years, we built a managed property network spanning from Garda to Lake Como, to the heart of Milan.

Today we manage complete property management, hotel-grade housekeeping, luxury linens and multilingual concierge for villas and apartments that are worth — and cannot afford — mediocre standards.
