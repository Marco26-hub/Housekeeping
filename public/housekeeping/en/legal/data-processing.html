Data Processing

Operational data processing notice

Detailed extension of the Privacy Policy. Last updated: 9 May 2026.

1. Operational scope

This notice describes the technical and operational data processing performed in the context of services delivered by The Blondes Cleaning to property owners, hosts and end clients.

2. Categories of data subjects

  • Owners of properties under management.
  • Guests staying at managed properties (only operational data needed for check-in/out).
  • Site visitors and contact requesters.
  • Telegram bot users (concierge MVP).

3. Data flows

  • Booking data: received from OTAs (Airbnb, Booking.com) according to their respective contractual terms.
  • Quality control data: internal photos and operational reports retained as proof of service delivery.
  • Communication channels: WhatsApp, Telegram, email — used only to manage requests and operations.

4. Anti-fraud security

Technical events such as connection logs, repeated access attempts and other security signals are processed under legitimate interest to protect the site, customers and data subjects.

5. Data subject rights

Rights under arts. 15–22 GDPR may be exercised at privacy@theblondescleaning.com. Response within 30 days.
