How orders are placed and confirmed

Steps from quote to active service. Last updated: 9 May 2026.

1. Quote request

The client submits a request via WhatsApp, the Telegram concierge bot, the contact form on the site or by phone. The Provider replies within 2 hours (Mon–Sat) with an initial assessment.

2. Free site visit

For management and recurring housekeeping, a free on-site visit is arranged to verify the property, confirm scope and define standards. For one-off housekeeping a remote quote may be issued based on photos and floor plans.

3. Quote & package

The Provider issues a written quote specifying:

  • Selected package (Signature 20% / Premium 25% / Elite 30% — or standalone housekeeping €/sqm).
  • Included services and any extras.
  • Operational SLAs (response times, turnover times).
  • Payment and invoicing terms.
  • Contract duration and termination clauses.

4. Order confirmation

The order is confirmed by signing the management or service agreement. The agreement is sent digitally and may be returned by digital signature. Once signed, the order becomes binding.

5. Onboarding

Typical onboarding takes 7–10 business days from order confirmation:

  • Property photo session (where applicable).
  • Listing optimization on OTAs.
  • Local team assignment and training.
  • Key handover with documented chain of custody.
  • Welcome kit and linen stock setup.
  • Initial deep-clean turnover.

6. Order changes

Changes to scope (added rooms, additional services, change of package) are submitted in writing and confirmed by the Provider with a written addendum to the agreement.

7. Standalone housekeeping orders

One-off or recurring standalone housekeeping orders can be scheduled:

  • By WhatsApp message at least 48 hours in advance.
  • Subject to availability of the local team.
  • Payment by bank transfer or invoice as agreed.

8. Cancellations

See Returns & Cancellations for service cancellations, contract termination and refund policy.
