Frequently Asked

Quick answers for owners and hosts

The essential info to understand if The Blondes Cleaning is the right partner for your property.

Which areas do you serve?

We operate on Lake Garda, Lake Como and Milan for short-term rental management, villa housekeeping, check-in, linens and concierge services.

How much does full management cost?

Management packages are Signature 20%, Premium 25% and Elite 30%. The percentage is confirmed in the quote based on the property, requested standard and included services.

Can I request standalone housekeeping and villa care?

Yes. Standalone Housekeeping starts at €5/sqm up to 70 sqm, €5.5/sqm from 70 to 140 sqm and €6/sqm over 140 sqm, with tailored quotes for complex properties.

Do you also handle linens and welcome kit?

Yes. Depending on the package we manage sheets, towels, laundry, basic, premium or elite welcome kits, and property preparation before guest arrival.
