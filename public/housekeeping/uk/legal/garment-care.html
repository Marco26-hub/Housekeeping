Garment & Linen Care

Care standards for linens, towels and garments

How linens are handled, washed and replaced — and the owner's responsibilities. Last updated: 9 May 2026.

1. Linen sets

Each managed property is provisioned with at least 3 full linen sets (sheets + pillowcases + duvet covers per bed) to ensure rotation between stays. Towel sets follow the same logic.

  • Signature: standard cotton, 220 thread count.
  • Premium: luxury cotton percale, 320 thread count.
  • Elite: sartorial linen and Egyptian cotton, premium GSM, ironed and folded by hand.

2. Washing protocol

  • Industrial washing at 60–90 °C with EN 14065 hygiene-controlled detergent.
  • Soft fabrics: dedicated cycles below 40 °C.
  • Sanitization of the wash batch between guests.
  • Stain pre-treatment before main cycle.
  • Drying in temperature-controlled industrial tumblers.

3. Quality standards

  • Replacement of any item showing visible wear, persistent stains or damage.
  • Folding and presentation according to luxury hospitality standards.
  • Sealed delivery of clean sets to the property.
  • Per-stay rotation traceability log.

4. Damages and stains

The Provider is not liable for damages caused by guests (irreparable stains, burns, tears). Such damages are documented with photos and reported to the owner. Replacement cost is charged to the guest's security deposit (where available) or to the owner via separate invoice.

Damages caused by the Provider's gross negligence (proven) are covered by the Provider's professional liability insurance.

5. Owner-supplied linens

Where the owner provides their own linens, the Provider washes and rotates them under the same protocol. The Provider is not liable for fabric deterioration arising from normal wear, manufacturing defects or pre-existing conditions.

Owner-supplied linens must be labeled with care instructions. Items without care labels are washed under the standard 40 °C cycle.

6. Garment care (concierge)

For Elite-tier clients, individual garment care for guests (light laundry, ironing, dry-cleaning coordination) can be activated as an extra service. Pricing is quoted per request based on volume and item complexity.

7. Prohibited items

The Provider does not handle:

  • Items requiring restricted couture-grade or museum-grade restoration.
  • Garments with embedded electronics or technical sensors.
  • Wedding gowns and ceremonial outfits without prior written agreement.
  • Items containing prohibited substances.

8. Returning items

Owner-supplied linens and personal garments are returned at end of contract, cleaned and folded, in sealed packaging. Items not collected within 60 days from end of contract may be considered abandoned and disposed of at the Provider's discretion.
