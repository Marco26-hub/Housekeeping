Cancellation, withdrawal and refund policy

Conditions for cancelling orders, terminating agreements and obtaining refunds. Last updated: 9 May 2026.

1. Right of withdrawal (consumer)

Consumers under EU law are entitled to a 14-day right of withdrawal from distance-signed contracts (art. 52 of the Italian Consumer Code, Legislative Decree 206/2005). To exercise the right, the client sends a written request to hello@swa-housekeeping.com within 14 days of contract signing.

If the service has already started before the 14-day period expires at the client's express request, the client owes a proportional amount for the services rendered up to that point.

2. B2B / professional clients

The 14-day statutory withdrawal does not apply to clients acting as professionals or businesses (host professionals, property companies). Cancellation conditions for B2B contracts are governed by the management agreement signed between the parties.

3. Service cancellation (one-off housekeeping)

  • More than 48 hours before: free cancellation, full refund.
  • 24–48 hours before: 50% of agreed fee retained as cancellation charge.
  • Less than 24 hours / no-show: 100% of agreed fee due.

Cancellation is sent by WhatsApp or email and is effective once acknowledged.

4. Termination of management agreement

The standard management agreement runs for 12 months and renews tacitly unless terminated. Either party may terminate by written notice with at least 60 days' prior notice before the end of the term.

Early termination for cause (material breach not cured within 30 days of written notice) is allowed under the conditions set out in the agreement.

5. Refunds

Refunds, where due, are paid by bank transfer to the IBAN provided by the client within 14 business days from the request being approved. Bank fees are at the receiving party's expense unless otherwise agreed.

6. Exclusions

No refund is due for:

  • Services already fully rendered and accepted (e.g. completed housekeeping turnover).
  • Personalized welcome kits already prepared and delivered.
  • Out-of-pocket expenses already incurred (linen wash batch, third-party suppliers).

7. Disputes & complaints

Complaints are sent to hello@swa-housekeeping.com with order reference. The Provider responds within 10 business days. For unresolved disputes, the parties may request mediation under Legislative Decree 28/2010 before judicial action.
