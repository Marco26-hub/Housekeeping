Shipping & Delivery

Delivery conditions

Delivery of welcome kits, linens, supplies and concierge purchases. Last updated: 9 May 2026.

1. Operational delivery

Linens, welcome kits and operational supplies are delivered to the managed property by the local team within the agreed turnover schedule. No shipping fee applies — costs are included in the management package or in the standalone housekeeping rate.

2. Concierge purchases

For Premium and Elite tier clients, the concierge service can purchase and deliver groceries, flowers, gifts or special requests to the property. Pricing:

  • Cost of items at receipt.
  • Concierge fee: 15% of cart value, minimum €25.
  • Out-of-town deliveries (more than 30 km from operational base): mileage charge at €0.50/km return.

3. Delivery times

  • Standard requests: within the same day if requested before 12:00, otherwise next morning.
  • Special items (wines, fresh flowers, hampers): 24–48 hours.
  • Bulky furniture or large appliances: agreed individually.

4. Geographic coverage

  • Lake Garda: Sirmione, Salò, Desenzano, Bardolino, Gardone Riviera and surrounding areas.
  • Lake Como: Bellagio, Cernobbio, Menaggio, Tremezzo and surrounding areas.
  • Milan: Brera, Quadrilatero della Moda, Porta Nuova, City Life, Navigli and central districts.

Deliveries outside these areas may incur additional charges and longer lead times.

5. Acceptance

Acceptance of perishables and high-value items requires presence of a person authorized by the owner or their delegate (housekeeping team). Delivery without acceptance is at the requester's risk.

6. Damage in transit

Goods damaged in transit must be reported within 24 hours of delivery, with photographic evidence. Replacement is processed where the supplier's policy allows; otherwise a refund is issued.

7. Force majeure

Delivery times may be affected by traffic conditions, weather, lake ferry schedules or supplier availability. The Provider notifies the client promptly of any expected delay.
